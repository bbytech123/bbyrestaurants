Built around restaurant operations

When the network has problems, the restaurant feels it.

A restaurant network is not just internet and Wi-Fi. Payments, handhelds, kitchen displays, printers, cameras, music, TVs, office computers, guest devices and building systems can all depend on the same infrastructure.

BBY designs that infrastructure as one system—then separates it into the right secure segments so each part gets the connectivity it needs without being unnecessarily exposed to everything else.
